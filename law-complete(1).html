What does COSMOS translate to? The Law.

You want to go to the Lawful place? The Heavens?

I am quite certain they prefer practitioners of Lawfulness in the Lawful place.

You think you're not missing out? Think it sounds too close to the word awful?

Well you're going to be Full of Awe when you see the coming of the Heavens.

You will say to the cliffs, "fall on us!"

"But Christ fulfilled the Law that means we--"

What? That means you what?

On Lawlessness

Lawlessness is seen as a direct opposition to God's will and His commandments. Jesus and other biblical writers strongly caution against it, warning of its destructive consequences.

Matthew 7:21-23
"Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven. Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity."
1 John 3:4
"Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law."

The law is not being abolished, not even when the book of Romans beautifully articulates the law.

The invention of rocket ships does not suddenly invalidate the law of gravity for everyone.

"Oh but love is the greatest law."

You know how to use "love" as laws greater every bit as much as you know how to use a rocket ship to overcome the law of gravity.
